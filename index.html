Our 2024 Japan Toolkit

🚄 1. The Explorer's Key: JR Pass & Pocket WiFi

You can't experience both worlds by just staying in Tokyo. The JR Pass is your golden ticket to ride the Shinkansen (bullet train) from the anime hubs of Tokyo down to the traditional heart of Kyoto and Osaka. Pair it with a Pocket WiFi—an absolute lifesaver for using Google Maps to hunt down obscure anime merch shops, navigating complex subway stations, and translating Japanese text on the fly.

Booking tickets on Japanese websites can be notoriously difficult. GetYourGuide is the easiest way to lock in both sides of your itinerary in English. 🛡️ 3. The Digital Shield: PureVPN

Here is the ultimate otaku dilemma: Japanese Netflix and streaming services often don't have English subtitles! PureVPN is our go-to hack. It allows you to bypass regional blocks so you can keep up with your seasonal anime on Crunchyroll or US Netflix while relaxing in your hotel room.
